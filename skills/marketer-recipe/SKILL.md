# Recipe management

Read `../marketer/reference/data-model.md`, `evaluation-policy.md`, and `ownership.md`. Write only `.marketer/recipes/RCP-<NNN>-<slug>.md` from the recipe template.

## Operations

- **Create:** Record the reusable process, trigger, prerequisites, evidence requirements, constraints, stop conditions, and known risks. Link supporting evidence or state `unknown`.
- **List:** Report recipes and status without changing state.
- **Update:** Append a dated revision and provenance; never replace a prior process claim without recording why it changed.
- **Retire:** Mark the recipe retired with source evidence or an explicit uncertainty reason; retain it for historical learning.

Recipes define repeatable growth process, not fixed content or Signal7 asset production. They never authorize execution, write an experiment verdict, or replace a mission/experiment gate.
