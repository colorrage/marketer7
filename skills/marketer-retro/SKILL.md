# Retrospective management

Read `../marketer/reference/data-model.md` and `memory.md`. Write only a new `.marketer/retros/R-<NNN>-<slug>.md` based on the retro template.

## Procedure

Review completed or paused mission artifacts and identify what worked, what did not, and what should change. Every claim must cite a mission, experiment, evidence, measurement, or evaluation path; distinguish weak confidence from a reusable conclusion.

Candidate reusable lessons remain in the retro until separately promoted with `marketer-memory`. Do not rewrite evidence, change terminal verdicts, or create a route decision through a retro.

Return `complete` with the retrospective path, or `needs_input` if no mission history exists to support the reflection.
